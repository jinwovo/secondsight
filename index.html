What this looks for

Honest notes

  • Nothing leaves this page. There is no backend, no analytics and no network request of any kind after the page loads. Open your network tab and watch it stay empty. Detection runs on your machine because the text you would most want to check is the text you would least want to upload.
  • It tries not to cry wolf. A joiner inside an emoji, a ZWNJ in Persian, a byte-order mark at offset zero and Cyrillic prose are all left alone on purpose. The control specimen in the gallery exists so you can watch it stay quiet -- a detector you never see silent is one you cannot trust when it speaks.
  • Reading intent is a heuristic. When a decoded payload looks like an instruction rather than a serial number, that is pattern matching and it is labelled as such. It raises a finding's severity; it never invents one.
  • It is not a decompiler. Zero-width steganography has no single standard. Known encodings are decoded and the rest is reported as present but undecoded, because a confident wrong answer is worse than an honest gap.
